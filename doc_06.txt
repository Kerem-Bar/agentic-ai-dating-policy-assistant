The compatibility scoring framework governs quantitative evaluation for candidate profiles that have successfully cleared initial categorical eligibility. Candidates violating any absolute screening dealbreaker defined in core eligibility receive a score of zero and are rejected with an immediate left swipe pass. Profiles satisfying baseline eligibility criteria enter the scoring matrix with a foundational baseline score of sixty points.

Positive evaluation credits reward educational synergy, athletic commitment, and lifestyle authenticity. Candidates holding an advanced academic graduate degree (master's or doctoral level), or actively pursuing graduate studies, receive an additional premium credit of fifteen points. Evidencing an intensive athletic lifestyle, demonstrated by structured athletic training, running, or gym dedication four or more times weekly, confers an additional credit of ten points. The presence of authentic candid photographs featuring domestic companion dogs or rescue pets provides a character signal conferring ten points. A thoughtful, articulate biography exhibiting wit and creative effort adds five points.

Decision thresholds determine final profile engagement actions. Candidate profiles accumulating a total composite score below seventy points are routed to an automatic left swipe pass. Profiles securing a composite score between seventy and eighty-four points qualify for a standard right swipe like. Profiles reaching or exceeding an elite composite score of eighty-five points qualify for a priority super-like designation.

In-app progression scoring governs the transition from platform dialogue to offline date coordination. Unlocking external contact channels, such as sharing a personal telephone number for dinner coordination, requires the candidate to maintain positive conversational decorum, adhere to active-hour response windows, satisfy the three-day and twenty-turn cadence threshold, and formalize a sit-down restaurant reservation.
