The candidate initiation window enforces immediate momentum following a mutual match. Once a match is successfully established, the candidate has an absolute window of twenty-four hours to initiate the conversation with an opening message. If twenty-four hours elapse from the moment of matching without any proactive communication from the candidate, the connection is classified as passive disinterest, resulting in an automated unmatch. Furthermore, all subsequent written communication during the initial seventy-two hours (three full calendar days) must remain strictly contained within the internal messaging interface of the dating application.

Transitioning communication to third-party platforms—including disclosing Instagram handles or exchanging personal telephone numbers and WhatsApp contact details—is strictly prohibited during the initial seventy-two hours. Off-platform migration is permitted only after fulfilling two cumulative milestones: completing at least three full consecutive calendar days of in-app interaction, and accumulating a minimum volume of twenty substantive, reciprocal conversational turns. In addition, personal telephone numbers are disclosed exclusively and strictly for the functional purpose of coordinating the logistical arrangements of an upcoming in-person date. Inquiries requesting off-platform contact prior to satisfying these thresholds, or requests lacking date coordination intent, must be declined immediately.

Messaging cadence operates strictly within daytime active hours between 07:00 and 23:00. Candidate responses delivered during active hours must arrive within an expected latency window of two to twelve hours. Sending messages during late-night and early-morning quiet hours between 23:00 and 07:00 constitutes an intrusive boundary violation and prompts an immediate unmatch, regardless of elapsed time. The twelve-hour response latency timer freezes entirely throughout quiet hours and resumes counting at 07:00. Latency exceeding twenty-four active daytime hours without prior contextual notice indicates disinterest and triggers automated unmatching.

Media discipline and conversational composure govern in-app boundaries. The transmission of unsolicited audio recordings or voice notes prior to an in-person meeting is strictly prohibited; transmitting a voice message without obtaining prior explicit written consent via text constitutes poor etiquette resulting in an immediate unmatch. Similarly, sending unsolicited photographs, personal images, or digital media files without advance written permission represents a severe privacy breach warranting an instant unmatch. In addition, sending multiple consecutive messages before the counterparty has responded to a prior substantive inquiry, commonly termed double texting, is explicitly forbidden.

Conversational momentum must progress intentionally toward an in-person meeting rather than degenerating into perpetual digital correspondence. If an interaction extends beyond five consecutive calendar days from the initial match, or accumulates more than forty conversational turns without the candidate formally proposing a specific sit-down dinner date and initiating venue coordination, the dynamic is classified as passive stalling. An interaction reaching this five-day stalling threshold warrants an immediate unmatch decision.
