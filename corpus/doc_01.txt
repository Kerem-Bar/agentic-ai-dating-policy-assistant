The candidate age framework strictly enforces an active screening bracket between twenty-seven and thirty-six years old. Any profile indicating a chronological age below twenty-seven or exceeding thirty-six constitutes an automatic rejection requiring an immediate left swipe. In addition, candidate profiles that disable the age display feature or conceal chronological age are disqualified instantly upon initial review.

Geographic eligibility is determined strictly by the candidate's declared permanent residential municipality, rather than real-time GPS proximity readings. The candidate's primary home residence must be situated within a maximum perimeter of fifteen kilometers from Tel Aviv, encompassing central municipalities such as Ramat Gan and Givatayim. Because dynamic GPS distance readings fluctuate due to workplace commutes, social outings, or visiting family, real-time proximity cannot substitute for residential status. A candidate currently located in Tel Aviv whose declared home residence is a distant municipality such as Nahariya is disqualified immediately with a left swipe pass. Conversely, a candidate whose verified permanent home is within the fifteen-kilometer perimeter remains eligible even if temporarily traveling outside the perimeter. Candidate profiles must explicitly designate a verified municipal residence; profiles concealing residential location data, omitting the home city, or listing a permanent residence exceeding fifteen kilometers are rejected upon initial review.

Primary visual screening demands complete transparency in the initial profile photograph. The lead cover image must exclusively depict the candidate as an individual; group photographs containing multiple individuals are strictly prohibited and warrant an immediate left swipe pass. Furthermore, the lead photograph must provide an unobstructed, sharp, and in-focus representation displaying both facial features and body frame together. Primary photographs featuring sunglasses, headwear or hats, blurred focus, or extreme distance framing are disqualified immediately.

Lifestyle habits represent a core screening layer. Active tobacco smoking in any form, including electronic cigarettes, vapes, and hookah, constitutes an absolute dealbreaker resulting in an immediate left swipe. Occasional social consumption of alcohol is fully permitted and incurs no negative penalty. In addition, candidates must maintain a consistent fitness routine, evidenced by regular participation in athletic sports, running, or structured gym training; complete absence of physical exercise results in immediate disqualification.

Educational attainment defines baseline cognitive and lifestyle synergy. A qualified candidate must hold a minimum of a recognized undergraduate academic degree (bachelor's level) or be actively enrolled and pursuing an academic degree program. Profiles reflecting a complete absence of higher academic education or active degree studies are rejected upon initial review.

Professional stability reflects vocational direction and personal autonomy. The candidate must maintain stable, gainful employment in an established professional discipline, demonstrating financial independence. Candidates actively enrolled as full-time academic students fulfill this requirement through their current studies. Complete professional inactivity, prolonged unemployment outside of degree studies, or enlistment as a mandatory military recruit results in an immediate left swipe.

Profile biography composition requires tangible self-presentation effort and descriptive substance. The written bio section must contain coherent, original text comprising at least eight to ten substantive words articulating personal interests, professional direction, or lifestyle values. Profiles presenting an entirely blank bio, displaying dismissive placeholder phrases (such as "just ask" or "not good at writing bios"), or consisting exclusively of external social media handles or URLs without substantive biographical text fail the effort standard and warrant an immediate left swipe pass.

Relationship goals and marital status require explicit alignment with serious, long-term romantic commitment upon initial inspection. Candidate profiles must explicitly display a stated relationship intent of "long-term relationship" (or platform equivalents specifying long-term partnership goals). Profiles that leave the relationship intent field blank, omit relationship goals, or list preferences such as "casual", "short-term", "figuring out my dating goals", or "intimacy without commitment" are disqualified immediately with an automatic left swipe pass. In addition, any profile indicating that the individual is currently married, actively separated without a final legal divorce, in an existing partnership, or pursuing non-monogamous dynamics (including explicit terms such as "open relationship", "ethical non-monogamy", "ENM", or "polyamorous") constitutes an absolute dealbreaker resulting in an immediate left swipe.
