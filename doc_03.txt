Initial greetings and conversational opening lines must exhibit substance and intentionality. Generic, low-effort openers consisting exclusively of single-word salutations, such as "Hey", "Hi", or standalone emojis without an accompanying open-ended question or substantive reference to profile interests, reflect low effort and trigger an immediate unmatch.

Linguistic precision and conversational effort serve as direct indicators of cognitive alignment and interpersonal respect. Candidate messages containing frequent grammatical errors, severe spelling mistakes, or heavy slang abbreviations (such as substituting single phonetic letters for full words) reflect inadequate care and warrant an immediate unmatch. Furthermore, beginning with the second conversational exchange, any candidate message consisting of fewer than five substantive words (such as "Cool haha", "Yeah sure", or "Nice") indicates low conversational investment and prompts an immediate exit.

Conversational familiarity and emotional pacing must remain strictly proportional to relationship depth. The premature deployment of pet names, romantic terms of endearment, or patronizing affectionate labels prior to an in-person meeting constitutes an immediate boundary violation. Any candidate using terms such as "babe", "baby", "honey", "sweetheart", "cutie", "gorgeous", or equivalent endearing nicknames during chat exchanges is classified as demonstrating artificial intimacy and must be unmatched immediately.

Conversational equilibrium requires active mutual curiosity and balanced dialogue progression. Interaction must not devolve into a one-sided interview where the user exclusively drives conversational inquiries while the candidate merely responds. Reciprocity is strictly monitored across conversational exchanges: if a candidate completes two consecutive messaging turns without directing a return question or inquiry back to the user (evidenced by an explicit interrogative question mark or a clear conversational question seeking the user's perspective, daily experiences, or interests), the interaction exhibits severe disinterest and warrants an immediate unmatch.

Prior relationship history and emotional availability constitute critical behavioral screening flags during conversational discovery. A candidate who emerged from a long-term romantic relationship lasting four consecutive years or longer must observe an elapsed post-breakup or separation transition period of at least six full months prior to entering active dating. Candidates revealing during conversation that they completed a relationship of four or more years without six full elapsed months since termination are classified as emotionally unready and must be unmatched immediately. Conversely, previous relationships spanning shorter tenures below four years carry no mandatory post-breakup waiting period.

Conversational romantic intent must remain strictly aligned with committed, exclusive long-term partnership goals, superseding any prior claims displayed on the candidate's public profile. If a candidate whose profile originally listed a long-term relationship goal subsequently indicates during the chat dialogue—directly or indirectly—that they are seeking casual dating, short-term encounters, physical intimacy without commitment, or phrases reflecting lack of serious readiness (such as "going with the flow", "seeing where things go without labels", "nothing heavy", or "just looking for fun"), the interaction constitutes an immediate intent contradiction. Verbal expressions of casual or ambiguous relationship intent during conversational discovery override public profile declarations and mandate an immediate unmatch decision.
