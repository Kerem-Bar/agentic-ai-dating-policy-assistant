First encounter proposals and venue criteria are governed by strict dining standards. Acceptable date proposals are restricted exclusively to formal, sit-down dinners at established restaurants featuring dedicated table service. Proposing casual, low-investment rendezvous—such as grabbing a takeaway coffee, walking in outdoor parks, meeting for drinks at a bar counter, or sitting on public benches—is categorically unacceptable and results in an immediate unmatch. In addition, proposing venues selected primarily because they are situated "nearby" or adjacent to the candidate's personal residence reflects low effort and self-serving convenience, triggering conversational termination.

Domestic boundary security and physical safety strictly regulate initial meeting environments. Any candidate proposal suggesting that the initial encounter take place inside a private apartment, residential home, hotel room, or isolated private setting constitutes a severe safety red flag resulting in an instantaneous unmatch. Initial meetings must occur exclusively within publicly populated commercial dining establishments during regular operating hours.

Scheduling protocol and advance planning maintain deliberate courtship standards while eliminating impulsive behavior. Date invitations must be coordinated with a minimum advance notice window of forty-eight hours; impromptu invitations proposed on the same day or day prior are rejected immediately. Furthermore, candidates inquiring whether the user is "spontaneous" ("Are you spontaneous?"), or attempting to arrange last-minute, unscripted encounters, are disqualified instantly, as this behavior reflects poor planning and an avoidance of structured coordination. The candidate is required to formalize the plan by securing a advance table reservation under his name at the designated restaurant.

Personal transportation independence ensures physical security and clear personal boundaries during the initial encounter. The user maintains full autonomy over arrival and departure logistics for all first encounters. Offers by the candidate to provide automobile pick-up, rideshare coordination, or personal vehicle escort from the user's home address prior to the date must be declined, requiring independent travel to and from the dining establishment.
